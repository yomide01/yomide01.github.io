Privacy Policy

Updated November 2026 · AAT Studio

AAT Studio ("we", "our", or "the Studio") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the security of creator credentials, channel analytics, and media assets.

Information We Collect

  • OAuth 2.0 Authorization Credentials: When an authorized creator connects a YouTube channel via Google OAuth 2.0, we receive scoped access and refresh tokens strictly for the authorized channel. We never collect or store account passwords.
  • Channel and Video Telemetry: Through the YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API v2, we retrieve channel-level statistics and per-video performance metrics (such as views, average view percentage, engaged views, and audience retention cohorts) for editorial optimization.

How We Use Information

  • To upload creator-approved vertical short-form videos, titles, descriptions, tags, and top-level engagement comments to the creator's authorized YouTube channel.
  • To generate internal editorial pacing diagnostics and retention reports for the channel owner.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AAT Studio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell, license, or share Google user data with third parties or advertising networks.

Credential Storage, Encryption and Revocation

All OAuth 2.0 refresh tokens are encrypted at rest using NaCl SecretBox (XSalsa20-Poly1305) authenticated encryption. Creators may revoke AAT Studio's access at any time directly via their Google Account Security Permissions page, as well as review the Google Privacy Policy.
