Privacy Policy

1. Scope & Purpose

AAT operates as an internal short-form video editorial workbench and channel publishing suite managed solely by its proprietor. This site does not collect, sell, or broker personal data from public visitors.

2. Client-Side OAuth Handshake

When an authorized operator performs a platform OAuth 2.0 redirect through the workstation handshake endpoint, parameters are read strictly within local browser memory so the operator can copy them to their private studio controller. No authorization codes or tokens are logged or transmitted to any third-party analytics server by this static site.

3. API Services & Revocation

Publishing workflows that interact with YouTube API Services adhere to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Operators may revoke application access at any time through their Google Security Settings.
